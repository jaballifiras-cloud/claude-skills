# Double-entry accounting

## Core rules
- Assets = Liabilities + Equity. Every entry has equal debits and credits.
- Normal balances: Assets and Expenses increase with **debit**; Liabilities, Equity, and Revenue increase with **credit**.
- Accrual basis: revenue when earned, expenses when incurred, regardless of cash.

## Chart of accounts (typical numbering — adapt to local standard)
1 Assets · 2 Liabilities · 3 Equity · 4 Revenue · 5 Cost of sales · 6 Operating expenses · 7 Other income/expenses.
Many countries mandate a national chart (e.g. the French-style plans used in France, Tunisia and Algeria use classes 1–7 in a different order: 1 equity/long-term liabilities, 2 fixed assets, 3 inventory, 4 third parties, 5 financial, 6 expenses, 7 revenue; Morocco uses its own 8-class plan). Ask which standard applies before designing one.

## Common entries (Dr = debit, Cr = credit)
| Transaction | Entry |
|---|---|
| Owner invests cash | Dr Bank / Cr Capital |
| Credit sale with VAT | Dr Receivables (gross) / Cr Sales (net) / Cr VAT payable |
| Customer pays | Dr Bank / Cr Receivables |
| Purchase on credit with VAT | Dr Purchases or Inventory (net) / Dr VAT receivable / Cr Payables (gross) |
| Pay supplier | Dr Payables / Cr Bank |
| Cost of goods sold (perpetual) | Dr COGS / Cr Inventory |
| Salaries | Dr Salary expense (gross) / Cr Employee withholdings & social security / Cr Salaries payable (net) |
| Depreciation | Dr Depreciation expense / Cr Accumulated depreciation |
| Accrued expense | Dr Expense / Cr Accrued liabilities |
| Prepaid expense used | Dr Expense / Cr Prepaid expenses |
| Deferred revenue earned | Dr Deferred revenue / Cr Revenue |
| VAT settlement | Dr VAT payable / Cr VAT receivable / Cr Bank (difference) |
| Bad debt provision | Dr Bad debt expense / Cr Allowance for doubtful accounts |
| Loan received / repaid | Dr Bank / Cr Loan — then Dr Loan + Dr Interest expense / Cr Bank |

## Depreciation
- Straight-line: (cost − residual value) / useful life.
- Declining balance: book value × rate (rate = multiplier / useful life).
- Units of production: (cost − residual) × units used / total expected units.

## Period-end close checklist
1. Bank reconciliations for every account.
2. Reconcile sub-ledgers (AR, AP, inventory, fixed assets) to control accounts.
3. Accruals, prepaids, deferred revenue, depreciation, provisions.
4. Inventory count and adjustments.
5. FX revaluation of foreign-currency balances.
6. Tax computations (VAT return, income tax provision).
7. Trial balance review → financial statements → lock the period.
8. Closing entries: revenue and expense accounts to retained earnings (income summary).

## Financial statements
- **Income statement**: Revenue − COGS = Gross profit − Operating expenses = Operating profit (EBIT) − Interest − Tax = Net profit.
- **Balance sheet**: current/non-current assets; current/non-current liabilities; equity.
- **Cash flow statement**: operating (indirect method: net profit + non-cash items ± working capital changes), investing, financing.

## Caution
Tax rates, legal charts of accounts, and reporting obligations depend on the country and change over time. State assumptions, ask for the jurisdiction, and recommend that the user confirms with a licensed accountant for legal filings.
